# Chronicles of the Sun Kings

An educational puzzle game that uses Tetris mechanics to teach African history to primary school learners.

## Overview

Chronicles of the Sun Kings follows the journey of a young apprentice Griot who must restore scattered fragments of African history. Through mastering puzzle challenges, players unlock achievements that reveal stories about ancient African civilizations, from the trade routes of West Africa to the stone fortresses of the South.

## Game Concept

The game bridges entertainment and education by making historical learning the reward for puzzle-solving mastery. Players organize falling blocks (representing scattered history fragments) to progress through levels and unlock educational content about African heritage.

## Features

### Two Game Modes
- **Story Mode**: Progress through levels to unlock achievements and historical content
- **Classic Mode**: Traditional Tetris gameplay without story elements

### Achievement System
- **The Golden Courts of Wagadugu** (Level 10): Learn about trade and commerce in West Africa
- **The Libraries of the North** (Level 20): Discover mathematics and architecture of ancient Egypt
- **The Stone Fortresses of the South** (Level 30): Explore engineering and unity in Great Zimbabwe
- **The Sankofa Bird** (Master Achievement): Complete all quests to restore the Sun-Scrolls

### Educational Objectives
- Introduce students to major African civilizations
- Connect historical events to specific African regions
- Highlight African innovations and achievements
- Promote the Sankofa philosophy: "It is not taboo to go back for what you have forgotten"

## Technical Details

- **Engine**: Godot 4.6
- **Language**: GDScript
- **Platform**: PC, Mobile (portrait orientation)
- **Target Audience**: Primary school learners (Ages 7-12)
