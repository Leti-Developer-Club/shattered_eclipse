# Chronicles of the Sun Kings
## Educational Tetris Game

---

## Slide 1: Game Overview

**Chronicles of the Sun Kings**

An educational Tetris game teaching African history to primary school learners.

- Classic Tetris gameplay with cultural storytelling
- Unlock tribal histories by progressing through levels
- Developed by Leti Arts Internship Team 5

**Target Audience:** Primary school students

---

## Slide 2: Game Modes

**Two Ways to Play**

**Story Mode**
- Progress through 5 levels
- Unlock 5 achievements about African tribes
- Save and continue your journey
- Complete the quest to earn the Sankofa Bird

**Classic Mode**
- Endless Tetris gameplay
- No achievements or story
- Pure puzzle challenge

---

## Slide 3: Educational Content

**Learn African History Through Play**

**4 Tribal Histories Unlocked:**
1. Anufo Tribe - Smithing village traditions
2. Ashanti Kingdom - Empire and Golden Stool
3. The Ga Tribe - Migration and festivals
4. The Fantes - Coastal region culture

**Master Achievement:**
- The Sankofa Bird - "Go back and fetch what you forgot"
- Unlocked after completing all tribal histories

---

## Slide 4: Key Features

**Engaging Gameplay Elements**

- Dynamic backgrounds change with each level
- Griot spirit guide character with animations
- Achievement book with detailed tribal histories
- Classic Tetris scoring system
- Multiple music tracks during gameplay
- Visual effects and sound feedback

**Technical Implementation:** Godot Engine 4.6

---

## Slide 5: Educational Impact

**Learning Through Gaming**

**Why It Works:**
- Gamification makes history engaging
- Progressive unlocking encourages completion
- Interactive storytelling keeps students interested
- Accessible gameplay for all skill levels

**Cultural Preservation:**
- Teaches African heritage to young learners
- Preserves tribal histories and traditions
- Promotes cultural pride and awareness

**The Sankofa Philosophy:** Learning from the past to build the future.
