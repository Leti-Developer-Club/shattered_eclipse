ACHIEVEMENTS UPDATE - STORY MODE RESTRUCTURE
==============================================

NEW STORY MODE STRUCTURE
------------------------
Story Mode now has 6 levels (0-5) with cultural/historical achievements:

LEVEL 0: Tutorial/Warm-up
- Requirement: Clear 3 lines
- Purpose: Introduction to gameplay
- No achievement unlock

LEVEL 1: Anufo Tribe (Smithing Village)
- Requirement: Reach Level 1 (10 lines cleared)
- Achievement: "The Anufo Smiths"
- Location: Dapaong and Mango areas (Togo/Ghana)
- Content: 3 pages about Anufo history, warfare, and royal lineage

LEVEL 2: Ashanti Kingdom
- Requirement: Reach Level 2 (20 lines cleared)
- Achievement: "The Golden Stool"
- Location: Kumasi, Ashanti Region (Ghana)
- Content: 3 pages about Ashanti empire, Golden Stool, and spiritual beliefs

LEVEL 3: The Ga Tribe
- Requirement: Reach Level 3 (30 lines cleared)
- Achievement: "Homowo Festival"
- Location: Accra region (Ghana)
- Content: 3 pages about Ga migration, Homowo Festival, and 20th century history

LEVEL 4: The Fante (Coastal Region)
- Requirement: Reach Level 4 (40 lines cleared)
- Achievement: "Fetu Afahye"
- Location: Cape Coast, Central coastal Ghana
- Content: 3 pages about Fante confederacy, traditional food, and Fetu Afahye Festival

LEVEL 5: The Sankofa Bird (Master Achievement)
- Requirement: Reach Level 5 (50 lines cleared)
- Achievement: "The Sankofa Bird - Master of Chronicles"
- Purpose: Final level to prove mastery
- Unlocks when all 4 tribal achievements are completed

==============================================

ACHIEVEMENT DETAILS
-------------------

1. THE ANUFO SMITHS (Level 1)
   Pages:
   - Page 1: Origin and identity - Anufo/Chakosi people from Anou on Komoé River
   - Page 2: Warfare tactics - Messenger system with pellets (war) or corn (peace)
   - Page 3: Royal lineage - Na/Nam prefix, Biemah and Somah commanders, royal gates

2. THE GOLDEN STOOL (Level 2)
   Pages:
   - Page 1: Ashanti people - 4.7M population, major Akan ethnic group
   - Page 2: Empire history - 17th century rise, Kumasi capital, gold trade dominance
   - Page 3: Golden Stool & spirituality - Sacred symbol, Nyame worship, ancestor reverence

3. HOMOWO FESTIVAL (Level 3)
   Pages:
   - Page 1: Migration history - From Lake Chad through Niger basin to Ghana
   - Page 2: Cultural identity - Homowo Festival, matrilineal/patrilineal descent
   - Page 3: 20th century challenges - Disasters, earthquakes, plague, emigration

4. FETU AFAHYE (Level 4)
   Pages:
   - Page 1: Fante identity - Coastal Akan, confederacy vs Ashanti, Mankessim capital
   - Page 2: Traditional food - Kenkey, Banku, seafood, yam harvest origins
   - Page 3: Fetu Afahye Festival - Disease remembrance, cleanliness, epidemic prevention

5. THE SANKOFA BIRD (Level 5)
   Master Achievement - Unlocked by completing all 4 tribal achievements
   Represents: "It is not taboo to go back for what you have forgotten"
   Meaning: Full restoration of the Sun-Scrolls and mastery of African history

EDUCATIONAL CONTENT SUMMARY
---------------------------

Anufo Tribe:
- Geography: Togo/Ghana border region
- Key Themes: Warfare, diplomacy, royal succession
- Cultural Elements: Military commanders, messenger system

Ashanti Kingdom:
- Geography: Central Ghana (Kumasi)
- Key Themes: Empire building, trade, spirituality
- Cultural Elements: Golden Stool, Nyame worship

Ga Tribe:
- Geography: Greater Accra region
- Key Themes: Migration, resilience, social structure
- Cultural Elements: Homowo Festival, dual descent system

Fante People:
- Geography: Central coastal Ghana
- Key Themes: Maritime culture, resistance, festivals
- Cultural Elements: Fishing traditions, Fetu Afahye
