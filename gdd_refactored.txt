1. GAME OVERVIEW

Chronicles of the Sun-Kings is an educational puzzle game that uses Tetris mechanics 
as "Knowledge Duels" to unlock African historical content. Players progress through 
levels, and upon reaching milestones, they unlock achievements that reveal stories 
about ancient African civilizations.

The game bridges entertainment and education by making historical learning the reward 
for puzzle-solving mastery.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2. CORE CONCEPT: TETRIS AS KNOWLEDGE RESTORATION

The Metaphor:
- Falling blocks = Scattered fragments of history
- Clearing lines = Organizing and restoring knowledge
- Level progression = Journey across the African continent
- Achievements = Recovered pieces of the Sun-Scrolls

This creates a meaningful connection between gameplay and narrative: players are 
literally "putting the pieces together" to restore lost history.

3. GAME MODES

STORY MODE

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4. PROGRESSION SYSTEM

LEVEL STRUCTURE
- Every 10 lines cleared = 1 level gained
- Levels 0-30 span the three main quests
- Speed increases with level (classic Tetris progression)
- Highest level reached is saved

SCORING SYSTEM (Classic Tetris Rules)
- Single line: 40 × (level + 1)
- Double (2 lines): 100 × (level + 1)
- Triple (3 lines): 300 × (level + 1)
- Tetris (4 lines): 1200 × (level + 1)

5. ACHIEVEMENT SYSTEM: THE THREE QUESTS

QUEST 1: The Golden Courts of Wagadugu (Levels 0-10)
Unlock Requirement: Reach Level 10
Historical Theme: Trade and Commerce in West Africa
Educational Content:
- The Ghana Empire and trans-Saharan trade routes
- Gold and salt trade economics
- The concept of fair exchange in African markets
- Cultural significance of Wagadugu (Ouagadougou)

QUEST 2: The Libraries of the North (Levels 11-20)
Unlock Requirement: Reach Level 20
Historical Theme: Mathematics, Architecture, and Scholarship
Educational Content:
- Ancient Egyptian mathematics and pyramid construction
- The Library of Alexandria and African scholarship
- Timbuktu's universities and manuscript tradition
- Astronomical knowledge in ancient Africa

QUEST 3: The Stone Fortresses of the South (Levels 21-30)
Unlock Requirement: Reach Level 30
Historical Theme: Engineering and Unity
Educational Content:
- Great Zimbabwe's stone architecture
- Engineering techniques without mortar
- The Kingdom of Zimbabwe and its trade networks
- Symbolism of walls representing community strength

MASTER ACHIEVEMENT: The Sankofa Bird
Unlock Requirement: Complete all three quests
Meaning: "It is not taboo to go back for what you have forgotten"
Reward: Full restoration of the Sun-Scrolls
Represents: Mastery of both gameplay and historical knowledge

6. THE STORY: THE SHATTERED ECLIPSE

PROLOGUE
For thousands of years, the history of the African continent was protected by the 
Sun-Kings. They held their power not in swords, but in the "Sun-Scrolls" - a 
collection of every innovation, proverb, and trade secret from North to South. 
This knowledge was kept in a magical golden jar, guarded by the Great Shaman, Muzu.

THE GREAT FORGETTING
A shadow known as "The Eclipse" grew jealous of this light. It shattered the golden 
jar, scattering the history of the continent to the winds. People forgot how to 
trade fairly in the West, how to build great stone walls in the South, and how to 
read the stars in the North. The world began to lose its color, turning grey and 
silent.

THE HERO'S JOURNEY
You play as a young apprentice Griot - the only one who can still see the faint 
"glimmer" of lost history. Muzu, now a spirit guide trapped within the jar's 
fragments, calls out to you. He gives you the Griot's Compass, which points toward 
lost knowledge.

THE RESTORATION
Through mastering the art of organization (Tetris), you prove your ability to 
restore order from chaos. Each level you conquer brings back a piece of history. 
Each achievement unlocked restores color to a region of the continent.

Your journey ends when you reassemble the Sun-Scrolls and earn the Sankofa Bird, 
proving that "it is not taboo to go back for what you have forgotten."

8. EDUCATIONAL OBJECTIVES

PRIMARY LEARNING GOALS
• Historical Awareness: Introduce students to major African civilizations
• Geography: Connect historical events to specific African regions
• Cultural Appreciation: Highlight African innovations and achievements
• Critical Thinking: Understand cause-and-effect in historical contexts

PEDAGOGICAL APPROACH
• Reward-based learning: History content as achievement rewards
• Progressive difficulty: Matches cognitive development
• Repetition through gameplay: Reinforces learning through practice
• Self-paced: Students progress at their own speed

THE SANKOFA PHILOSOPHY
"It is not taboo to go back for what you have forgotten"
- Encourages reviewing learned material
- Removes shame from asking questions or revisiting content
- Promotes iterative learning and mastery
- Culturally grounded in Akan wisdom

CONCLUSION

Chronicles of the Sun-Kings successfully bridges the gap between entertainment and 
education by using Tetris as a metaphor for restoring scattered history. The 
achievement-based progression creates clear goals while the Sankofa philosophy 
encourages iterative learning.

The reduced scope makes the project achievable while maintaining educational value. 
The foundation is solid for future expansion into a more comprehensive educational 
experience.
